GHL Expertise

Automation Workflows I Build

Real workflows I've designed and deployed — from booking sequences to full ecommerce fulfillment pipelines.

Studio Placement Booked Call Sequence
Triggers when a customer books via calendar. Auto-tags as "Booked Call", creates/updates opportunity in CRM pipeline, then ends.
290U Application Qualification
Form-triggered dual-branch workflow: qualifies leads, sends acceptance email + "Qualified" tag, or routes to soft disqualification/nurture with a 24h delay.
Connecting GHL to Whop
Inbound webhook creates a contact, then routes via condition into 4 branches: Main $97, Upsell $47, Downsell $27, or None — each adds a product tag.
CKSR Low Ticket Purchased
Contact created trigger → checks for existing tag → if not tagged, adds low ticket tag → sends Email → waits 1 min → SMS → wait 24h (multi-touch follow-up).
Paid Whop Onboarding
Dual trigger (contact created + tag check) → creates opportunity → adds tag → Welcome Email → Onboarding Email 1 → Wait → Onboarding Email 2 → End.
Purchase — Close Opportunity Won
Fires when "TSA Trading & Investing Group" tag is added → immediately closes opportunity as Won in the CRM pipeline. Clean, instant pipeline update.
